Research Areas

Research

The laboratory focuses on theoretical and computational studies of quantum transport, topological states of matter, and nanoscale nonequilibrium phenomena.

01
Condensed Matter Theory

We investigate collective electronic phenomena in solids, including topological phases, graphene-based systems, and van der Waals heterostructures.

02
Quantum Nano Theory

We study quantum transport, Coulomb blockade, Kondo physics, electron-vibration coupling, and nanoelectromechanical shuttle devices.

03
Topological Matter

We explore bulk-boundary correspondence, Chern insulators, topological flat bands, non-Hermitian topology, exceptional points, and PT-symmetric lattices.

04
Quantum Technology

We investigate Cooper-pair flying qubits, nanomechanical cat states, superconducting proximity effects, and theoretical platforms for quantum information processing.

05
Out-of-Equilibrium Physics

We describe transport and dynamics of nanoscale systems under thermal and nonthermal driving, including kicked currents, interferometry, and spin-current generation.

06
Graphene and 2D Materials

We study strain, nanobubbles, valley isospin, interface tunneling, and quantum-dot formation in graphene, MoS2, h-BN, and related two-dimensional materials.
